| 3. after | ivfflat.probes = 10 | recall@10 = **1.0000** (good), 0 warning(s) left |

The diagnose suggestion produced in step 2 was:

> Raise it with SET ivfflat.probes = 10 and measure the effect with vector_index_recall_check(bench_ivf_idx).

**Honest limitation of this section.** The measured recall was **1.0000** at
`probes = 1` *and* **1.0000** at `probes = 10`. On this synthetic uniform
dataset the IVFFlat scan returned the exact top-10 in both cases (its scan
touched roughly the whole relation either way), so **this section does not
demonstrate a probes-driven recall regression** — it demonstrates that the
diagnose path works for IVFFlat and warns on `probes = 1`.
Reproducing a probes-sensitive case needs a realistic clustered dataset
(production embeddings are clustered); we could not do that here, so no
number is claimed for it.
